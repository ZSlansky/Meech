Let's make a game!
  name:Meech Clicker

Includes
  *include 

Buttons
  *meechMine
    name:Meech Mine
    desc:Work the mines for Meech Money.
    on click:get 1 meechMoney
      if ((meechMoney:max)>0)
        show meechGacha
      end
    end

  *meechGacha
    name:Pull Meech Gacha 
    desc:Click to pull a Meech. Costs 10 Meech Money.
    hidden
    on click: 
      if (meechMoney > 9) 
        if ((have tenpull) and (meechMoney>=100))
          if (have tpdiscount)
            lose 90 meechMoney
          else
            lose 100 meechMoney
          end
          gain 10 meech
        else
          lose 10 meechMoney
          gain 1 meech
        end
      else
        toast You need more Meech Money.
      end
    end
    
Resources
  *meechMoney
    name:Meech Money
    desc:Used to buy things.
    show earned

Settings
  background:https://static.toiimg.com/thumb/msid-99612775,width-400,resizemode-4/99612775.jpg
  building cost increase:110%

Buildings
  *meech
    name:Meech
    desc:The default meech.
    on tick:get .1 meechMoney

Upgrades
  *meechagers
    name:Meechagers
    desc:Meechagers to manage your Default Meech, doubling production.
    passive:multiply yield of meech by 2
    cost:10000 meechMoney
    req:meech > 9
  *tenpull
    name: Ten Pull
    desc:Pull 10 meeches from the Gacha at once! Perfect for an impatient meech. 
    req:10 meechMoney
    cost:100 meechMoney
  *tpdiscount
    name: Ten Pull Discount
    desc: Pulling 10 meech at once costs 10% less.
    cost: 1000 meechMoney
    req:tenpull
  *pony
    name:Defeat the pony
    desc:Unleash the full power of an immense Meech Army and defeat 1 pony to beat the game.
    cost:1e+308 meech

Achievements
  *meech1
    name: Your first meech.
    desc: You pulled a Default Meech!
